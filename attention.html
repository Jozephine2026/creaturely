Creaturely · an anatomy · iii

attention

how the pieces of a sentence notice each other

The first two pages of this glossary follow a sentence into the space of positions: the model cuts the sentence into tokens, gives each token a vector, and can now measure and trace relationships between words. But every vector so far is a resting position, the word’s average meaning across everything the model read. The word beam has one resting vector whether the sentence is about light or about oak. Reading means adjusting those meanings to fit the sentence they are actually in. The mechanism that does the adjusting is called attention.

i · the noticing

Attention works like this: every token looks at every other token in the sentence, including itself, and gives each one a score for how much it matters right now. The scores are scaled so that they add up to exactly one. Think of a fixed budget of notice, which each word divides among its neighbours. These scores are called attention weights, and they are numbers the model calculates, not rules anyone wrote. The model can only attend to what sits inside its context window: the stretch of tokens it can see at once.

Below is this glossary’s sentence, a line from the Photonic Emergence poem. All of the arcs are present all of the time, every word attending to every word at once; press any word to bring its own share of the web forward.

a budget of notice

press a word.

Look at what echo does: it spends most of its notice on beam and sent, because an echo needs a source, and almost none on a or of. Small words spend their budgets too; even the is busy finding out what it belongs to. And every word keeps a little notice for itself. Nothing here is grammar the model was taught. The weights come from the vectors, and the vectors came from the training.

ii · the moving

The weights are not the point; they are the recipe. After scoring, each token updates its own vector by mixing in the vectors it attended to, in proportion to their weights. A token that spends most of its notice on beam ends up carrying some of beam’s meaning. This is the moment the map from the first page finally moves. After attention, a word’s position is no longer its resting average. It is a version adjusted to this sentence. Watch what a sentence does to beam.

the same word, moved by two sentences

beam rests between its two lives. read a sentence.

Same word, same token number, same resting vector, and two different arrivals. In the first sentence, echo and sent pull beam toward light; in the second, oak and roof pull it toward timber. The moved position is called a contextual meaning. It is not stored anywhere. The model rebuilds it every time it reads, from the weights and the mixing. This is also why the order and company of your words matter so much when you write to a model. You are not choosing labels; you are arranging the forces that will move every word’s position.

Attention runs many times over, not once. The model scores and mixes, then scores and mixes again on the results, layer after layer, and by the upper layers the tokens carry meanings assembled from the whole sentence. What the machinery of those layers is made of comes next.

in the poems

The poems’ habit of echo and return is attention made audible: every new word of a line was written while attending to all the words before it.

What the model notices is a habit it learned from other people’s sentences.

an honest note

The attention weights on this page are set by hand to illustrate the idea; a real model calculates them from the vectors themselves, using pieces called queries and keys that this glossary leaves aside. Real attention also runs many separate patterns at once, called heads, and repeats across dozens of layers, so no single set of weights decides anything alone. The map of beam’s movement compresses all of that into one step, and its positions are hand-placed. The flowing particles are an illustration of mixing, not a picture of anything physical moving inside the model. The budget-of-one is real: attention weights genuinely sum to one for each token.
